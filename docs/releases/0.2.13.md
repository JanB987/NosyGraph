## NosyGraph 0.2.13

This patch makes YAML root-link changes appear automatically in open graphs
and lenses, and makes the plugin settings easier to navigate.

### Fixed

- Adding a note link to a property listed in `rootNodeProperties`, or in the
  plugin's default root-node properties, updates open graph views and lenses.
- Root-link changes are processed even shortly after a graph-state save,
  rather than being discarded during the refresh-suppression window.
- Newly configured roots are merged into open and restored Lens layouts while
  preserving existing node positions, pinning, and expanded badges.

### Improved settings

- Group everyday options into Graph defaults, Appearance, Linked-note
  indicator, and Interaction.
- Put advanced note identifiers and YAML property mappings in collapsible,
  keyboard-accessible sections with clearer descriptions.
- Use theme-aware sections, aligned controls, labeled identifier fields, and
  a stacked layout for narrow panels.
- Hide the unused Graph marker, Filters, and Recursive property mappings.
  Connected Base filters remain available.
- Move Relationship direction into Legacy compatibility and explain its
  arrow-direction fallback. Existing settings and mappings are preserved.

### Compatibility and validation

- Minimum Obsidian version remains **1.5.0**.
- 560 tests across 104 files pass, together with the scoped typecheck,
  production build, lint, and whitespace checks.
- Local browser previews verify light, dark, and narrow layouts, control
  availability, and keyboard operation of the disclosure sections.
- Native Obsidian smoke tests and the external Obsidian plugin scan remain
  maintainer checks; local previews do not establish native-app parity.

### Update

Use Obsidian's community plugin updater, or replace `main.js`,
`manifest.json`, and `styles.css` in `.obsidian/plugins/nosygraph/`.
Keep your existing `data.json`, then reload NosyGraph.
